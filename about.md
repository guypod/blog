I'm Guy Podjarny (Guypo), and I'm the founder & CEO of [Tessl](tessl.io), where we are reimagining software development for the AI era and shaping AI Native Development. I previously founded and am on the board of [Snyk](https://snyk.io/), which created and leads the Developer Security category, and is now a multi-billion dollar company with over 1,000 employees. Before that, I was CTO at Akamai (following its acquisition my first startup, Blaze), and before that a developer and product manager. I'm also an angel investor, and the co-host of the [AI Native Dev podcast](https://tessl.io/podcast).
